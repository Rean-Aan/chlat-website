This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and protect the personal information of our users and visitors to our website through accessing or using the Website, Services, Apps and/or Content of Chlat App.

## 1. Information We Collect

In providing services, we may collect the following information:

**Information of parents, guardians or adults:** When you create an account, we will collect personal information such as name, date of birth and phone number.

**Information about your child:** We also collect information about your child, including the name, age or grade and the name of their school, through a parent or guardian. We do not collect this information directly from child users.

## 2. How We Use Personal Information

We use the personal information we collect to provide services that support the improvement of our products and services. In particular, we may use the information we collect to:

Analyze to understand how services are accessed and used and how they work. Therefore, we can improve the design and functionality to demonstrate the effectiveness of our services by researching, developing, supporting and improving our services and other educational products. We also perform analysis of aggregate data that does not identify individual users.

## 3. Transparency and Governance

We understand and respect your privacy concerns on Chlat App. Therefore, we give you the right to control your information. You can edit or delete information in your user account.

## 4. Child Privacy

We do not allow children under the age of 13 to create accounts, and we do not intentionally collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent and guidance.

For children under the age of 13 to use Chlat App, they need to use an account created by a parent, guardian or adult.

## 5. How We Store and Protect Your Information

We use designed measures and techniques to protect the accuracy and security of the personal information collected and to protect against unauthorized access or disclosure. Unfortunately, no data transmission or storage system can guarantee 100% security. If you have reason to prove that the security of your account has been leaked, please notify us immediately.

## 6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to change or modify our privacy policy from time to time.

The word "last update" at the top of this page indicates the date the policy was last modified.

Your use of the Services after these changes means that you accept the revised Privacy Policy.

## 7. Contact Us

If you would like to contact us with any questions or suggestions, please email us at <contact@chlat.app>.
